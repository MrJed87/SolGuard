UV figures and burn times are estimates and a guide only, not medical advice. Real exposure varies with cloud, shade, altitude, skin condition, medications and how sunscreen is applied. For official sun protection times see the Bureau of Meteorology and ARPANSA. People of all skin types can be harmed by UV. See your GP about any new or changing mole or skin spot.
